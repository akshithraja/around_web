Support

Help with the Aroundbee app.

Getting in touch

Email us at support@aroundbee.com. We reply within 2 business days.

If you can, include your username and a screenshot or screen recording of what you're seeing. It often makes the difference between us being able to answer and not — a lot of issues are hard to identify from a description alone.

Common questions

I don't see any trails or spots near me

Aroundbee is location-based and works wherever you are. Most posts right now are in Bengaluru, so if your area is quiet, tap the location at the top of the Trails or Spots tab and choose Bengaluru to see what's there. Choosing Your location switches back to wherever you are. And if your area really is empty — post one yourself and be the first Bee around you.

How are Top rated places picked?

Top rated places and food on Home come from Google Maps for the area you're in. Only your area is sent to us; sorting by distance happens on your phone, so your exact location isn't used to pick the list.

I didn't get my verification code

Aroundbee currently supports Indian mobile numbers only. Enter your 10-digit number without the +91, which is already filled in. Codes arrive by SMS and can take a minute — if nothing arrives, wait a few minutes before requesting another, then write to us.

My upload didn't finish

Photos and videos upload while you're in the app, so stay on the screen until publishing completes. Leaving the app or losing your connection partway can interrupt the upload, and you may need to create the post again. If you need to stop, tap Save draft first — drafts are saved when you choose to save them, not automatically.

Making a post private

Each trail and spot has a Visibility setting when you publish. Public posts can be seen by everyone on Aroundbee. Private posts are only visible to you and the people you give access to. You can pick those people while publishing with Share with people, or later from the ⋯ menu on the post in your profile → Access.

Following people

Tap Follow on someone's profile to see their new public trails and spots in your Following feed. There's no approval step. To unfollow, tap Following, then Unfollow. Only you can see who you follow and who follows you; others see just the counts. There's a daily limit on how many people you can follow.

Reporting content or blocking someone

Every trail, spot and profile has a ⋯ menu with Report and Block. Blocking hides that person's posts from you, stops them messaging you, and removes any follow between you. Manage who you've blocked in Settings → Account settings → Privacy → Blocked users. Reports go to our moderation team.

Too many notifications

Settings → Account settings → Notifications has four separate switches: Social (messages, follows, likes and saves), Following (new trails and spots from people you follow), New around you (new trails and spots nearby), and Reminders & nudges.

Deleting your account

Profile → Settings → Account settings → Delete account. You can tell us why you're leaving if you'd like (it's optional), then verify your phone number with a 6-digit code. This permanently deletes your account and your data, and it can't be undone.

Privacy and your data

To request a copy of your data or raise a privacy concern, email support@aroundbee.com. You can also switch off usage analytics at any time in Settings → Account settings → Privacy → Share usage analytics, or by emailing us.
