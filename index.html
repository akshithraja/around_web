TWO WAYS TO LOG

Trails and Spots.

TRAILMultiple places

A whole day out, stop by stop.

A food walk, a weekend trip, a game night. Log every stop in order, and your places, distance and time add up on their own.

SPOTOne place

That one place worth remembering.

A cafe, a view, a stall you stumbled on. Drop it as a Spot so you, and the people around you, can find it again.
